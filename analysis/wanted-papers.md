# Papers wanted, ranked by what they would settle

Links marked ✓ are ones I've actually seen resolve in search results; the rest are constructed from
citations and may need a title search.

---

## Tier 1 — would change conclusions in this repository

### 1. Abbott, Ewbank, Edwards & Wang (1998) — **the single most valuable**
*Molecular characterization of some enigmatic Lower Devonian fossils.* Geochim. Cosmochim. Acta
62:1407–1418. doi:10.1016/S0016-7037(98)00078-7

- ✓ https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0016703798000787
- ✓ https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/10153/ (Cardiff repository — may hold a PDF)

**Settles:** whether the *Prototaxites* pyrolysate contains **nitrogen heterocycles** (indole, pyrrole,
nitriles, diketopiperazines). Their presence supports the aromatic-protein hypothesis; phenols without
them supports the polyphenol reading. Also carries **¹³C solid-state NMR**, which separates amide
carbonyl from aromatic carbon directly — the most informative unread measurement on this organism.

### 2. Loron et al. (2026) — **Supplementary Materials**
Sci. Adv. 12(4):eaec6277. ✓ https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aec6277

**Settles:** **table S1**, the band definitions. Every quantitative claim I've made from their feature
matrix — the Amide II/Amide I ratio, the nitrogen-vs-aliphatic decomposition, the tectin gap
calculation — carries the caveat that I don't know their integration limits. This removes it. Also
figs. S4 (perylene) and S8 (macerate spectra), and their stated data tables.

### 3. Holman et al. (2024)
*Significance of lignin and fungal markers in the Devonian (407 Ma) Rhynie Chert.* Geobiology
22(4):e12616. doi:10.1111/gbi.12616

- ✓ https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/gbi.12616
- ✓ https://www.researchgate.net/publication/383267418

**Settles:** whether the H-type lignin monomer dominance is bulk chert or attributable to any specific
fossil — the attribution error in [`literature-sweep.md`](literature-sweep.md) Correction 2. If they
resolved it by fossil type, the tyrosine argument either recovers its footing or loses it definitively.

## Tier 2 — would sharpen the comparison

### 4. Bowser & Bernhard (1993)
*Structure, bioadhesive distribution and elastic properties of the agglutinated test of Astrammina
rara.* J. Eukaryot. Microbiol. 40(2):121–131.
(Search by title — I have not verified a direct link.)

**Settles:** whether the disulfide cross-linking is the whole story or whether aromatic cross-links
were also looked for. Currently the only direct evidence on monothalamid cross-link chemistry, and it
points away from di-tyrosine.

### 5. DeLaca (1986)
*Determination of benthic rhizopod biomass using ATP analyses.* J. Foraminiferal Res. 16(4):285–292.
(Cushman Foundation / GeoScienceWorld — search by title.)

**Settles:** the actual protein/carbohydrate/lipid percentages for *Astrammina rara*, **separating
protoplasm from structural test organics**. The only quantitative composition for a large
monothalamid, and the source of the disputed ~19% lipid figure.

### 6. Meyvisch et al. (2023) — **Supporting Information**
J. Phycol. 59(5):1064–1084. ✓ https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jpy.13382

**Settles:** **Table S2**, the 216-spectrum dinocyst dataset, and Table 1's band assignments. Would
allow a protist resistant-wall comparison group two orders of magnitude larger than the n = 3 amoeba
control.

### 7. Ní Fhlaithearta et al. (2013)
*Molecular and isotopic composition of foraminiferal organic linings.* Mar. Micropaleontol.
✓ https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0377839813000741

**Settles:** the actual guaiacol/syringol abundances in foram linings, for comparison against the
H/G/S distribution — the one direct lignin-marker comparison between forams and *Prototaxites*.

## Tier 3 — context and open threads

### 8. Boyce et al. (2003)
*Chemical evidence for cell wall lignification and the evolution of tracheids in early Devonian
plants.* Int. J. Plant Sci. 164:691–702. ✓ https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/377113
**Carbon XANES** on Devonian material — worth checking whether *Prototaxites* was included.

### 9. Honegger, Edwards, Axe & Strullu-Derrien (2018)
Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B 373:20170146.
✓ https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rstb/article/373/1739/20170146/
The asci/hymenium description. Needed to assess whether the propagules are spores or miniature tests
with apertures — the one place the hypothesis could gain positive evidence.

aliphatic-only. Citation not yet recovered; it is in their reference list.

## If only three

**1, 2 and 3.** Abbott 1998 for the pyrolysate and NMR; the Loron SI for table S1; Holman 2024 to fix

or close it.
